## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Simple independent marketing demo for Pasha Barbershop in Plano. Help visitors find services, reach the shop, and book through its existing Booksy listing. The demo is not the official shop website unless adopted.

## Users

Assumed audience: local customers considering a haircut or grooming appointment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the Booksy destination, phone, address, directions, and useful service information. Existing Vite static site deployed through Railway. Keep scope and maintenance small. Restore editable source HTML; current repository assembles generated fragments.

## Brand Commitments

User requests a complete redesign, distinctly different from Fresh Me Up. Black and gold reflect the shop's shirts. Turkish identity may inform restraint and craft; do not use a fez or red-hat motif. Plain, natural copy using Humanizer. User chose visual mockups for approval before coding.

Booksy listing checked October 1, 2026: https://booksy.com/en-us/1061273_pasha-barbershop_barber-shop_36433_plano . Services include haircuts, hair and beard, Turkish Delight, and Signature Service. Prices and hours must be checked against the current listing. Do not preserve synthesized testimonials as real customer quotations.

## Product Principles

- Make booking and directions easy to find.
- Keep the site small and specific to this shop.
- Do not invent customer reviews or shop photography.
